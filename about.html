Who We Are

A Local Hospital You Can Rely On

Madina Hospital is located in Surhi, on the Dumri–Bermo–Jaina Road in Jharkhand. We serve the surrounding community with round-the-clock availability, offering care that is dependable, affordable and close to home.

Our hospital is open 24 hours a day, with 24×7 emergency service and ambulance availability, so patients and families always have somewhere to turn — day or night.
